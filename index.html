Questions

Everything you wanted to ask.

What is demifine jewellery?

Demifine jewellery sits between fast-fashion and fine jewellery. Slayyshine pieces use 316L medical-grade stainless steel with a 2.5–5 micron PVD gold bond, so they look and last like fine jewellery at an everyday price — most pieces cost ₹699 to ₹1,799.

Is Slayyshine jewellery waterproof?

Yes. Every piece is waterproof. You can wear it in the shower, at the gym, in the pool and to bed. PVD gold is fused to the steel at a molecular level, so water and sweat do not wash it off.

Will PVD gold jewellery tarnish or turn my skin green?

No. Skin turns green when cheap brass or copper reacts with sweat. Our base metal is 316L stainless steel and the gold layer is PVD bonded, so it does not tarnish, fade or discolour skin with normal wear.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. 316L stainless steel is the grade used in medical implants. Our jewellery is hypoallergenic and contains zero nickel, the most common cause of metal allergies.

What is the difference between PVD gold and gold plating?

Regular gold plating is a thin wash of about 0.1 micron that rubs off in weeks. PVD (Physical Vapour Deposition) bonds gold at 2.5–5 microns in a vacuum chamber — up to 50 times thicker and far more durable.

How long does delivery take and is shipping free?

Orders ship within 2–3 days across India. Shipping is free on orders above ₹999, and returns are easy.

How should I care for my jewellery?

Very little care is needed. Wipe with a soft cloth to restore shine and store pieces separately to avoid scratches. Avoid harsh abrasives; water, sweat and everyday wear are fine.
