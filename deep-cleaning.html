FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a deep clean take? +

Usually 6–9 hours, depending on size and condition. For larger homes we send two crews so the job is finished in a day.

Do I need to be at home? +

Only at the start and end, to walk through the home with the supervisor. Many customers step out while we work.

How often should I deep clean? +

Every 3–6 months for most homes, with regular cleaning in between.
